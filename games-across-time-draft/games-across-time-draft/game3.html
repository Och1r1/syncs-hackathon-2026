How the race is run

A line of anklebones is laid out as the course and each rider takes one bone as a horse. Riders cast four shagai in turn and move by what lands face up — only the horse moves a rider, one stone for each that shows. Camel, sheep and goat leave you where you stand.

Дөрвөн бэрх — all four bones landing on four different faces — is the lucky cast: four steps and another throw. Four of a kind does the same.

Prototype note

Step values and face odds here are illustrative and simplified for this prototype — real anklebones are not evenly weighted, and house rules vary across the steppe. Verify with cultural sources before treating any of this as authoritative.
